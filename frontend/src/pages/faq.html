Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, flight tracing, hotel hourly slots, and cancellations.

✈️ How does flight delay protection work?

We integrate real-time flight tracking APIs. If your incoming flight is delayed, our system automatically informs your designated driver and updates your hourly transit hotel check-in slot. You do not lose booking hours or money.

🛂 Do I need a visa to exit the airport?

Yes. If you exit the airport terminal gates to board cabs, check-in to hotels, or eat at restaurants outside, you must hold a valid Indian Tourist or Transit Visa. Double-check requirements at Transit Visas Guide.

🏨 Can I book a transit hotel for just 3 hours?

Yes. Unlike standard travel portals that force overnight bookings, LayoverX offers hourly Day-Use slots (3-hour, 6-hour, 12-hour, or overnight slots) so you only pay for the exact transit hours you need.

🚖 Where do I meet my cab driver?

All pre-arranged transfer drivers meet passenger exits at T2 Gate 2 arrivals level. Your chauffeur details (name, vehicle number, phone) arrive via WhatsApp and SMS 2 hours prior to landing.

💳 What is the cancellation policy?

We offer flexible bookings. Cancel free up to 3 hours before your scheduled check-in slot. Cancelations within 3 hours incur a 50% booking fee. No-shows are charged in full.
