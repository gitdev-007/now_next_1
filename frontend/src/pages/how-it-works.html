Indian Transit Visa Rules

To exit the airport grounds during layover, travelers must hold a valid Indian visa. For transit stays under 72 hours, you can apply for an Indian e-Transit Visa online at least 4 days prior to departure. Alternatively, standard e-Tourist visas are accepted for exits.

Apply on Government Portal

Left Luggage Locker Facility

Don't drag heavy luggage around Mumbai. Clear security with your cabin bags and store larger check-in bags at the CSMIA Terminal 2 arrivals locker desk (operated by airport authorities). Rates range from ₹150 to ₹300 per bag depending on luggage size.

📍 Location: Arrivals Hall, Ground Level

Transit General FAQs

What happens if my guide or cab causes me to miss my flight? ▼

We maintain a Missed Flight Guarantee. Because we calculate strict time-buffers and track real-time traffic updates via our dispatch system, we have a 100% on-time flight return track record. If a delay under our driver's control causes you to miss a flight, LayoverX covers rebooking costs up to $1,500.

Where is the left luggage facility in Mumbai Airport? ▼

The luggage lockers counters are located in the public arrivals area of Terminal 2 (Level 2 Arrivals, near the exit gates). Look for signs marked "Left Luggage" or ask any CSMIA airport executive. It is open 24/7.
