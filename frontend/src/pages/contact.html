How Can We Help?

Can I cancel my transit booking if my flight path changes? ▼

Yes! We support 100% free cancellations up to 2 hours prior to your scheduled flight landing. Simply send us your booking confirmation ID via WhatsApp or Email support to trigger an immediate refund.

How do I register a corporate travel partnership? ▼

Please reach out directly to our business desk at partners@layoverx.com. We offer corporate discount APIs, custom billing, and VIP meet-and-greets for executive transit groups.
