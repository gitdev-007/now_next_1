Important Guide

How to Meet Your Driver at CSMIA T2

1

Clear Immigration

Proceed to immigration. Clear passport control using your transit visa (approx. 20-30 mins wait time).

2

Exit Gate 2

Collect baggage if checked in, then exit the arrival hall and head straight toward Exit Gate 2.

3

Spot Name Placard

Your driver will be waiting in the arrivals placard zone with a LayoverX banner showing your name.

Airport Taxi FAQ

What happens if my landing flight is delayed? ▼

We track your flight number in real-time. If your flight is delayed or reaches early, our pickup schedule adjusts automatically. There are zero extra charges for flight delays.

Do these quotes include Bandra-Worli Sea Link tolls? ▼

Yes! All taxi quotes shown are flat-rate and include highway tolls, airport terminal entry park fees, and driver allowances. The price you book is the final price.
