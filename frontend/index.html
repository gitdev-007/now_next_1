Help & Support

Need Answers? We're Here to Help.

Find solutions quickly with our comprehensive help center. From check-in to checkout, we’ve got you covered.

Browse Help Center
Do I need a transit visa to leave Mumbai airport?
Most international transit passengers exiting the terminal will require a transit visa or tourist e-Visa for India. Passengers exiting Terminal 2 (T2) can obtain e-Visas online in advance. LayoverX helps provide guidance instructions, but we highly recommend checking current Indian immigration directives.
What happens if my inbound flight is delayed?
Our system continuously tracks flight arrivals. If your inbound connection is delayed, our system automatically recalculates safety margins, updates the suppliers, and notifies your assigned chauffeur. If your layover window shrinks below safe thresholds, we will prompt or refund non-feasible segments.
Where is my luggage stored during the stopover?
International baggage checked through to your final destination usually stays on the aircraft. For carry-on items or luggage checked only to Mumbai, CSMIA Terminal 2 features verified Left Luggage cloakrooms. Our airport transit guides walk you directly to these facilities.
How do you calculate safe layover buffer windows?
We mandate a strict safety buffer: 2 hours subtracted for custom clearance and immigration exit checks, plus another 3 hours for boarding check-ins and security lines. This leaves active stopover hours calculated cleanly to protect your flight connection.
How much does a private transfer from T2 to city center cost?
Private airport transfer costs vary by vehicle type and distance. Sedan transfers to most Mumbai hotels typically start from ₹1,200 including tolls and driver allowances. SUVs and luxury executive sedans are priced from ₹2,000 and ₹3,500 respectively. All prices are flat-rate and include flight tracking to ensure your driver meets you promptly.
