Airport Hotels FAQ

Do I need an Indian Visa to stay at these hotels? ▼

If you book a hotel inside the airport terminal (like Niranta inside T2 Transit area), you do not need an Indian visa. If you book an external hotel (even 1 km away), you will need to clear immigration, which requires a valid Indian Transit Visa or e-Tourist Visa.

How do hourly transit check-ins work? ▼

Unlike standard hotel check-ins (usually 12 PM or 2 PM), transit hotels support check-ins at any hour of the day or night. Your booking duration (3h, 6h, or 12h) starts the moment you check in at the front desk.

Are airport shuttles free? ▼

Most 5-star hotels near CSMIA (like JW Marriott Sahar, Hilton, and Orchid) include free airport pick and drop shuttles as part of their day-use packages. Mid-range hotels may charge a nominal taxi fee (approx. ₹300-₹500). Please check details in the cards.
