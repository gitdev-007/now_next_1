Experiences FAQ

What happens if my flight is delayed? ▼

Don't worry! We monitor flight arrivals in real-time. If your flight is delayed, we automatically shift your guide and private pickup schedule. If the delay makes the tour impossible, we offer a 100% immediate refund.

Where does the driver pick me up? ▼

For Terminal 2 (International), the driver will meet you outside Terminal Arrivals Exit Gate 2 with a personalized placard showing your name. For Domestic Terminal 1, they meet you at the central pick-up zone. Complete coordinate contacts are sent 1 hour prior to landing.
