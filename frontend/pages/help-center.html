🛂 Indian Transit Visa Requirements

If you plan to leave the airport terminal during your layover, you must hold a valid Indian Visa.

  • Transit Visa: Valid for up to 72 hours of stopover, must be applied online at least 4 days before flight departure.
  • e-Tourist Visa (30 Days): Double entry, ideal for stopovers, lets you explore the city comfortably.
  • Exemptions: Passengers remaining inside the airport terminal transit hotel (e.g. Niranta in T2) do not require an exit visa.

🧳 Left Baggage & Locker Service

Don't drag heavy luggage around Mumbai. Left luggage counters are available at Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (CSMIA) Terminals 1 and 2.

  • T2 location: Arrival Hall (ground level), near taxi exit gates.
  • Operational hours: 24/7 round the clock.
  • Rates: Hourly options starting from ₹150 per standard bag. Secure tags and receipts are provided.

🛫 Step-by-Step Terminal Exit Process

Exiting Mumbai airport takes approximately 45 to 75 minutes from landing:

  1. De-board: Follow signs to Immigration Hall.
  2. Border Check: Go to the designated "Transit Visa" or "e-Visa" lanes.
  3. Left Luggage: (Optional) Deposit baggage at arrivals locker bay.
  4. Meet Driver: Walk to Exit Gate 2. Your LayoverX driver will be holding a placard with your name.
