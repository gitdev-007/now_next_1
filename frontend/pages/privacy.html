Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and secure all passport and transit passenger flight details.

1. Information Collection

When you book stopover activities on LayoverX, we collect the following details:

  • Lead passenger name & nationality.
  • Passport number (required for airport terminal gate customs verification checks).
  • Incoming & outgoing flight numbers (used for auto-tracking delays).
  • Emergency contact number.

2. How We Use Information

Your contact details, flight numbers, and passport identifiers are shared securely with:

  • Your designated chauffeur cab operator.
  • The transit hotel or spa salon check-in desks.

We do not rent, sell, or profile your passenger details to third-party marketing firms.

3. Local Storage & Security

Temporary booking and current itineraries are stored in your local web browser's `localStorage` cache. Logged-in profiles store encrypted copies securely in Google Cloud Firestore.
