Transit Dining Info

Dining FAQ

How much time do I need for a dining transit? ▼

We recommend a minimum layover of 4 hours to clear immigration, take a cab to nearby dining spots (1-3 km), enjoy your meal, and return for the mandatory 2-hour security check-in buffer.

Is the street food safe for international transit flyers? ▼

Yes! We only partner with and list highly hygienic, corporate-rated local dining centers (like Elco and Highway Gomantak) that use purified mineral water for preparations, ensuring zero health risks prior to long flights.
